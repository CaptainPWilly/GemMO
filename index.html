Five slots. 64 gems. One copy of each gem per Sack.

How your sack works

Red matches deal damage; blue matches grant Guard for every build. Each color has one shared reservoir. Its capacity is the sum of all equipped items' costs in that color. Matching adds charge once; any item of that color can spend from it. For example, several different Red gems share one combined Red pool. Unused-color charge is lost. Wilds substitute for any tile in a line of 3 or more with at least one non-Wild tile; they never clear a color or the entire board. Every active uses your turn; Quickstep uses that turn for a swap. Overdrive cannot stack. Same colors can stack, but the same exact gem cannot be equipped twice. These are provisional sidegrades for playtesting.

ENVIRONMENT · RIFT

Hazard

Each Environment match resolution deals 1 damage to both fighters, regardless of line length. Environment is shared danger, not Sack charge.

WILD · SUBSTITUTE

Any Color

A Wild can stand in for any normal tile inside a horizontal or vertical match. Wild-only lines do not count. A Wild is removed only when it participates in the matched line.

Board law

MATCH 3+MATCH 4 = EXTRA TURNMATCH 5 = WILD

  • Swap adjacent tiles. A normal swap must create a legal match.
  • Matches resolve, columns refill, and any cascades belong to the same fighter.
  • A line of 4 or more gives the player an extra action. A line of 5 or more forges a Wild.
  • If the board has no legal move, it reshuffles while preserving HP and charge reservoirs.

Your Sack

  • You equip five gems. Same-color builds are allowed, but each exact gem can appear only once in a Sack.
  • Matching a color fills that color's shared reservoir only if your Sack contains that color. Capacity is the sum of the equipped unique gems' charge costs.
  • Activating a gem spends its cost from the shared reservoir. Unspent charge remains for another gem of the same color.
  • An active gem normally replaces your board move unless its text explicitly grants another action.

Gem behaviors

  • Immediate: damage, Guard, healing, or another effect happens now.
  • Boardcraft: choose a tile, row, or column and change the board itself.
  • Attunement: activate a gem to bend a board rule for several of your actions. Every qualifying match resolution can trigger it, including cascades. Re-activating refreshes the duration; it does not stack.
