    ✦ FIELD NOTES
    THE BOARD

    Gemology

    Equipped gems hold reusable spells, techniques, or focuses. Matches charge them; activating one uses its bound ability. A healing spell and a healing potion can coexist because they use different resources.

    RED · ATTACK

    Strike

    Match your weapon’s color to deal its Attack per gem value. Red support gems amplify strikes, regardless of weapon color.

    BLUE · DEFENSE

    Guard

    Blue matches charge Blue gems. Guard comes from abilities, skills, and equipment; a Blue weapon also makes Blue matches deal damage.

    GREEN · SUPPORT

    Spells

    Green matches charge reusable support magic: healing, cleansing, regeneration, and growth effects. The gem is the focus; the ability is the spell.

    YELLOW · UTILITY

    Charge

    Yellow has no universal combat effect. It charges equipped Yellow gems built around tempo, tricks, and board utility.

    PURPLE · ARCANE

    Charge

    Purple has no universal combat effect. It charges stranger effects: Wilds, Overdrive, siphoning, and other rule-benders.

    GOLD · LOOT

    Treasure

    Gold matches add to your fight's Gold tally. Gold is not an ability reservoir.

    XP · PROGRESS

    Experience

    XP matches add to your fight's XP tally. Encounter-specific environmental effects can attach to XP gems instead of adding another board type.

    WILD · SUBSTITUTE

    Any Color

    A Wild can stand in for any normal tile inside a horizontal or vertical match. Wild-only lines do not count. A Wild is removed only when it participates in the matched line.

    Board law

    MATCH 3+CASCADE = CHARGE OPENERMATCH 4 = EXTRA TURNMATCH 5 = WILD

    • Swap adjacent tiles. A normal swap must create a legal match.
    • Matches resolve, columns refill, and any cascades belong to the same fighter.
    • The colored gems in Combo 1 become the chain anchors. Colored gems destroyed directly by a gem-breaking ability also count as that move’s Combo 1 anchors. Combo 2 adds +1 core value to each anchor, Combo 3 adds +2, Combo 4 adds +3, and so on. The ability’s normal initial reward rule still applies, and cascade charge does not retrigger ability-style match procs.
    • A line of 4 or more gives the matching fighter an extra action—player or enemy. A line of 5 or more forges a Wild.
    • If the board has no legal move, it reshuffles while preserving HP and charge reservoirs.

    Both Sacks

    Enemies equip real gems and follow the same weapon, charge, ability, Guard, cascade, and extra-turn rules. Tap their weapon badge or gems to inspect their loadout.

    • You equip up to five gems. Same-color builds are allowed, but each exact gem can appear only once in a Sack.
    • Matching a color fills that color's shared reservoir only if your Sack contains that color. Capacity is the sum of the equipped unique gems' charge costs.
    • Your equipped weapon makes matching gems of its color deal damage. Other colors charge abilities and apply their own effects. Red support amplifies weapon strikes, Blue prevents damage, Green restores health, Yellow creates tempo, and Purple controls the board or enemy charge. Guard comes from abilities, skills and equipment. One weapon can be equipped at a time unless an effect grants another weapon slot.
    • Activating a gem spends its cost from the shared reservoir. Unspent charge remains for another gem of the same color.
    • An active gem normally replaces your board move unless its text explicitly grants another action.

    Gem behaviors

    • Immediate: damage, Guard, healing, or another effect happens now.
    • Boardcraft: choose a tile, row, or column and change the board itself.
    • Attunement: activate a gem to bend a board rule for several of your actions. Each attunement triggers at most once per action; cascade-anchor bonuses do not trigger it again. Re-activating refreshes the duration; it does not stack.
