How your sack works

Red matches deal damage; blue matches grant Guard for every build. A match's charge is shared equally by equipped gems of that color. Five reds each receive one fifth of the red charge—not five times the power. Unused-color charge is lost. Full gems still take their share. Every active uses your turn; Quickstep uses that turn for a swap. Overdrive cannot stack. These are provisional sidegrades for playtesting.
