How your sack works

Red matches deal damage; blue matches grant Guard for every build. Each color has one shared reservoir. Its capacity is the sum of all equipped items' costs in that color. Matching adds charge once; any item of that color can spend from it. For example, five 7-cost reds share a 35-point pool. Unused-color charge is lost. Wilds substitute for any tile in a line of 3 or more with at least one non-Wild tile; they never clear a color or the entire board. Every active uses your turn; Quickstep uses that turn for a swap. Overdrive cannot stack. These are provisional sidegrades for playtesting.
